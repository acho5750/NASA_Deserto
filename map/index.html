Why this map

AI data centers need land and water

Companies are pouring money into new data center campuses. Each one converts land and depends on a local water supply.

This map shows how much land past campuses took, which water sources they depend on, and what announced projects could add by 2035, so residents and water planners can see it before campuses are built.

NISAR radar

What NASA's NISAR adds

NISAR is a NASA-ISRO radar satellite. It sees through clouds, day or night, and passes over every 12 days.

Its long L-band waves show new buildings rising and tiny ground motion over the aquifers campuses depend on. Anything deep teal on the map is NISAR.
