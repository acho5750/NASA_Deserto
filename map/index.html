About the data and methods

Footprints. Converted acres at built campuses come from Sentinel-1 radar (Planetary Computer sentinel-1-rtc): a pixel counts as converted once its backscatter changes by more than a threshold for two months in a row, inside the campus outline plus 200 m.

Projection. Acres and water for 2026-2030 use ratios measured at built sites (acres per MW, water per kWh from Google's per-site reports, build time) and each campus's stated MW or investment. Low, median and high use the 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles.

Water sources. Each campus is matched to the EPA water service area it sits in, then to the utility's source (EPA SDWA). Trends are SWOT water surface elevation (rivers) or OPERA DISP-S1 ground motion over aquifers. A trend describes the water body; it does not show what caused the change.

Shares. "Local share" divides the campus water estimate by the utility's own supply (population served x per-capita use). Where a utility buys its water, the "wholesale share" divides by the supply of the whole system it buys from.

Exposure. Land cover before construction: NLCD. Wetlands: USFWS NWI. Protected land: USGS PAD-US 4.1 (GAP status 1-3).
